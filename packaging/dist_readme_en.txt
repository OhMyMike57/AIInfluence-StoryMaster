AI Influence: Story Master  v{VERSION}

[Install] This is a regular Bannerlord module: put the whole
AIInfluence_StoryMaster folder into "Mount & Blade II Bannerlord\Modules"
and tick it in the launcher (after AI Influence and Harmony).

[Editor] It is StoryMaster.exe in this folder (Tool) - no install, just run it;
you can also launch it from the in-game MCM settings page ("Open editor").

- The editor's settings, terminology cache and backups live in
  %APPDATA%\AIInfluenceStoryTools (separate from the module folder), so
  updating the module (delete folder, drop in the new version) never loses them.
- The editor works on the AI Influence mod's save data; before editing, exit the
  campaign back to the main menu.
